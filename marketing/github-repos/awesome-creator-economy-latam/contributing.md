# Contributing to Awesome Creator Economy LATAM

Thanks for helping. Please follow these rules before opening a pull request:

## What belongs here

A resource belongs in this list if it is:

1. **Operationally relevant to LATAM creators.** It must accept LATAM currencies, operate in LATAM countries, be available in Spanish or Portuguese, or be specifically built for the region.
2. **Currently active.** No abandoned products, no broken links.
3. **Useful.** Not just "exists" — must materially help a creator, publisher, or developer building in the LATAM creator economy.

## What does NOT belong

- Paid placements or sponsored listings.
- Affiliate links.
- Resources that mention LATAM but don't operate there in practice.
- US/EU-only platforms unless they have meaningful LATAM presence.
- Self-promotion of products you have not personally evaluated.

## How to add a resource

1. Fork the repo.
2. Add your entry in the correct section, alphabetical within the section when possible.
3. Format: `- [Name](https://url) — One sentence describing what it does and why it matters for LATAM creators.`
4. Open a pull request with a clear title and a one-paragraph rationale.

## Review process

Pull requests are reviewed weekly. Editors check:
- Link works.
- Resource is LATAM-relevant.
- Description is neutral and informative (no marketing language).
- No duplicate entries.

## Code of conduct

Be respectful. Disagreements are fine; insults are not.
