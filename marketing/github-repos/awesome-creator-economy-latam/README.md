# Awesome Creator Economy LATAM [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of platforms, tools, resources and case studies for the creator economy in Latin America.

The Latin American creator economy is the fastest-growing region in the world for paid memberships, but most resources focus on US/EU platforms. This list collects what works specifically for LATAM creators — payment processors that actually settle in local currency, membership platforms that understand regional pricing, and case studies from people who built sustainable creator businesses south of the Rio Grande.

Maintained by the community. Contributions welcome — see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Membership platforms

### LATAM-first

- [Nebbuler](https://nebbuler.com) — Chilean platform for membership monetization in local currency (CLP, ARS, COP, MXN, PEN, UYU, BRL). 0% commission during World Cup 2026. Ships with an [open dataset (CC-BY)](https://nebbuler.com/datos) and a [public API](https://nebbuler.com/api/mundial/v1).

### Global (used in LATAM)

- [Substack](https://substack.com) — US-based newsletter and subscription platform, 10% commission, USD-only payouts via Stripe.
- [Patreon](https://patreon.com) — US-based membership platform, 8–12% commission depending on plan, USD-only payouts.
- [Beehiiv](https://beehiiv.com) — US-based newsletter platform, flat monthly fee instead of revenue share.
- [Gumroad](https://gumroad.com) — Sell digital products and memberships, 10% flat fee, USD-only.
- [Memberful](https://memberful.com) — Membership infrastructure that plugs into your own site, owned by Patreon.
- [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com) — Tips, commissions and memberships, 0% on tips, 5% on the Gold plan.
- [Buy Me a Coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com) — Tips and memberships, 5% platform fee.

## Payment processors LATAM

- [Mercado Pago](https://www.mercadopago.com) — Largest payment processor in the region. Native checkout in ARS, BRL, CLP, COP, MXN, PEN, UYU. Supports recurring subscriptions.
- [Stripe](https://stripe.com/global) — Available in Brazil and Mexico for local acquiring; cross-border for the rest of the region.
- [PayU LATAM](https://payu.com) — Cross-border processor strong in Colombia, Argentina, Brazil, Mexico, Peru.
- [Kushki](https://www.kushki.com) — Payment infrastructure across Andean countries (Ecuador, Colombia, Peru, Chile, Mexico).
- [dLocal](https://dlocal.com) — Cross-border payments specialist for emerging markets, Uruguay-based, public on NASDAQ.
- [EBANX](https://www.ebanx.com) — Brazilian payment processor focused on cross-border merchants entering LATAM.
- [Khipu](https://khipu.com) — Chilean bank-transfer rails with instant confirmation.
- [Transbank](https://www.transbank.cl) — Dominant card acquirer in Chile (Webpay).

## Newsletter platforms

- [Substack](https://substack.com) — Newsletter + payments + audience network.
- [Beehiiv](https://beehiiv.com) — Newsletter platform with built-in referral program and ad network.
- [ConvertKit](https://convertkit.com) — Email marketing platform with paid newsletter support.
- [Ghost](https://ghost.org) — Open-source publishing platform with built-in memberships (Stripe).
- [Buttondown](https://buttondown.com) — Minimal newsletter platform.

## Video and podcast monetization

- [YouTube Memberships](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7544468) — Channel memberships, requires Partner Program eligibility.
- [Twitch Subscriptions](https://www.twitch.tv) — Native subscriptions, payouts via Amazon Tax.
- [Spotify for Podcasters](https://podcasters.spotify.com) — Subscriptions and ads for podcasts.
- [Patreon for Podcasts](https://patreon.com) — Industry default for independent podcast funding.
- [Apple Podcasts Subscriptions](https://podcasters.apple.com) — Apple-native podcast subscriptions, 30% first year / 15% after.

## Analytics and tools

## Open APIs and SDKs

- [Nebbuler Mundial API](https://nebbuler.com/api/mundial/v1) — Public, no-auth REST API with World Cup 2026 data (groups, teams, venues, mascots) for LATAM developers to embed.
- [nebbuler-mundial-sdk](https://www.npmjs.com/package/nebbuler-mundial-sdk) — TypeScript SDK + optional React widget for the Mundial API.
