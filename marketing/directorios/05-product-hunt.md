## First Comment del Founder (storytelling 200 palabras)

```
Hey Product Hunt! Juan Pablo here, founder of Nebbuler.

Two years ago I watched my friend Diego — a journalist in Bogotá with 22k newsletter subscribers — try to turn his audience into income. He spent 3 months building on Substack. Three months of work for 31 paying subscribers. 0.14% conversion.

The problem wasn't his content. The problem was that his readers couldn't pay him. Substack charges in USD. A Colombian fan sees "$5/mo," his bank slaps a 3% international fee, the checkout warns him about foreign transactions, and 95% of them bail.

Patreon, Beehiiv, Memberful — same story. None of them accept MercadoPago. None of them charge in pesos. The biggest creator economy market outside the US is essentially locked out of the biggest creator tools.

So I built Nebbuler. It's what Substack would look like if it were designed in Santiago instead of San Francisco:

→ Native payments in 6 LATAM currencies
→ MercadoPago, Khipu, PSE, OXXO out of the box
→ Newsletter + paywall + member area + podcast hosting
→ 0% commission first 6 months, 5% after
→ Spanish-speaking humans answer your support tickets

187 creators signed up in our soft launch. Today we go live to everyone.

If you're a LATAM creator, or you know one drowning in USD checkouts — would love your feedback. AMA below 👇

Free to start: nebbuler.com
```
